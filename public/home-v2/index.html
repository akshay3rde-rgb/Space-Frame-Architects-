Indore’s summer is not a problem. It is a brief.

Shade, cross-ventilation and thermal mass do most of the real work, quietly, for the fifty years a building will stand after the client has forgotten which contractor installed the compressor.

    (06) The studio

    We walk the site before we measure it.

    Founded in 1999 after five years at a Mumbai practice, SpaceFrame set out to prove that central India’s institutions and homes deserved the same rigour as its metros. The founder still visits every site personally, twice a month, until the keys are handed over.
